### 👋 Hey, I'm Ryan

**Operations & Strategy Manager** turned **AI Engineer** — building intelligent agents and internal tools at the intersection of biotech and AI.

I come from a decade in life sciences (Broad Institute → NIH → biotech startups) and now I spend my days wiring up LLMs, building automations, and making scientists' lives easier. I believe the next wave of biotech breakthroughs will be driven by people who speak both biology and code.

---

### 🔧 What I'm Building

- 🤖 **AI Agent Ecosystem** — Personal and enterprise agents using MCP integrations, FastAPI backends, and LLM orchestration
- 🔬 **Biotech Automation** — Streamlining research program ops with AI-powered workflows
- 🧪 **Research Tools** — Internal apps for scientists using Cursor, Claude Code, and Python
